Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about working with Alphatron.

How does the website development process work?

We begin with discovery, prepare a proposal and quotation, agree on project terms, complete onboarding, build, review, launch and hand over the project.

How much does a website cost?

Pricing depends on scope, complexity, content, integrations and requirements. Contact us for a discovery conversation and quotation.

Do I need a domain already?

No. Alphatron can assist with domain registration and hosting where required.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We can assess the existing site and recommend improvements based on your business goals.

How long does a website take?

Timelines vary by project. The agreed timeline is documented in the proposal and depends partly on how quickly required content, feedback and approvals are provided.

What happens if I want changes during development?

Changes are reviewed against the agreed project scope. Out-of-scope changes may require a formal change request, additional cost or additional time.

What happens after my website launches?

You receive a structured handover. You can also choose an ongoing Digital Care arrangement for continued support.

Do you work with clients outside Zimbabwe?

Yes. Alphatron can work remotely with suitable clients outside Zimbabwe, subject to project requirements and practical payment and communication arrangements.
