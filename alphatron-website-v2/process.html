Our Process

A structured journey from first conversation to professional handover and ongoing care.

How we work

From idea to launch

Our workflow is designed to reduce confusion and keep both Alphatron and the client accountable.

1

Discover

We learn about your business, audience, goals, requirements, timeline and the problem you're solving.

2

Plan

We turn discovery into a clear solution, scope, timeline and quotation.

3

Build

Once approved and onboarded, development begins.

4

Review

Feedback is collected through the agreed review process. Out-of-scope changes are handled separately.

5

Launch & Handover

The completed project is approved, delivered and handed over using a structured checklist.

6

Care

Clients can continue with Digital Care for agreed maintenance and support.

Before we build

Discovery comes first.

A client request is not automatically a project scope. We establish the business need before committing to deliverables.

Clear scope. Clear expectations. No surprises.

Our internal workflow connects discovery, proposal, agreement, onboarding, delivery, change control, handover and closure.
